Demonstration for GRF-TAFC

GRF-TAFC: a Grounded and Reflective Framework for Task-Adaptive Formation Choreography of CAVs.
GRF-TAFC integrates context-grounded formation generation, optimization-based execution, and execution-driven reflection into a closed loop.
